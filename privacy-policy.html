Guestimator Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Guestimator is a game where you estimate how many dots you see. It is made by Justin Lawson, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Guestimator mobile app and to the browser version of the game.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your email, real name, phone number or location.
  • The game stores a random anonymous player ID, the display name you choose, and your gameplay results so that your progress and the leaderboards work.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and we don't sell your information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

InformationWhat it isWhy we use it
Anonymous player ID A random identifier created automatically the first time you play. It is not linked to your name, email or device identity. Keeps your progress and ranking tied to you across sessions.
Display name The name or nickname you choose for the leaderboards. Shows your name on leaderboards.
Gameplay data Your guesses, scores, rounds and session history, streaks, Daily Challenge results, and your Calibration rating and class. Runs the game, calculates rankings, shows your stats, and checks results for fairness.
Data stored on your device Settings and game results that are waiting to sync when you are offline. In the browser version this is kept in your browser's local storage. Lets the game work between sessions and send results once you are back online.

When your device connects to the game's online services, those services also receive standard technical information that any online service receives, such as your IP address and basic device and connection details. This is handled by our service provider, described below.

What we do not collect

  • Your email address, real name, phone number, or postal address
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers. The game shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To provide the game: saving progress, ranking players, and showing leaderboards and Daily Challenge results.
  • To keep the game fair, for example by validating submitted results.
  • To fix problems and keep the service running.

Leaderboards and display names

Your display name, ranking and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. Please don't use your real name or any personal information as your display name. We may remove or change names that are inappropriate.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

The game uses Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies, to handle sign-in, save your progress and run leaderboards. Unity acts as a service provider that processes this data on our behalf. Unity's own handling of information is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of players or the game.

Storage and security

Information sent between the game and its online services is encrypted in transit. Your data is stored on servers operated by our service provider, which may be located in a country other than your own. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information described here.

How long we keep information

We keep your gameplay data and display name for as long as the game is in operation or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can stop using the game at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes the data stored locally on it, but not the data saved to the online services.

To have your online data deleted, email jmlawson2002@gmail.com and tell us the display name you used and the platform you played on (Android or web). We may ask for a little more detail to confirm which record is yours. We will delete your data and reply within 30 days. Once deleted, your progress and ranking cannot be restored.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to object to or restrict how it is used, or to receive a copy of it. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children

Guestimator is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided a display name or other information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes, for example if we add account sign-in features. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the game or on its store page where appropriate.
